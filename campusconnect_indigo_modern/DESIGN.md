## Brand & Style

The design system projects energetic clarity, academic credibility, and modern digital fluency tailored for Indian collegiate ecosystems. It sits at the intersection of consumer app speed (Swiggy/Zomato), career-building ambition (Unstop/LinkedIn), and contextual workspace order (Notion).

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Vibrant Digital Minimalism**: Ultra-clean base canvases layered with electric lavender accents, high-contrast typography, and purposeful micro-interactions.
- **Tone & Mood**: Optimistic, authoritative yet approachable, youthfully ambitious, and structured. It balances chaotic multi-stakeholder needs (administrative notices, quick mess/canteen orders, internship drives, and peer societies) into high-legibility viewports.
- **Indian Context Adaptations**: Native support for the Indian Rupee (`₹`) symbol integrated at proportional optical heights, compact yet airy density modes for multi-tiered academic schedules, and high-contrast status signifiers that remain legible under direct sunlight on varying mobile screens.

## Colors

The system uses an intentional layered palette designed to keep complex portals readable without sacrificing youth appeal.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **App Background**: `#FAFAFE` — Crisp, cool-tinted off-white avoiding clinical starkness.
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — Pure white, providing high contrast against the app canvas.
- **Surface Hover / Subtle**: `#F4F3FB` — Subtle lavender-gray for row hovers, subtle banners, and active selections.
- **Card Stroke / Dividers**: `#E8E7F3` — Hairline tinted slate for crisp structural separation without heavy border weight.

### Brand Accents
- **Primary Violet**: `#6C5CE7` — Primary brand identifier, primary buttons, dynamic highlights.
- **Primary Dark / Hover**: `#5842D8` — Hover state for solid buttons and active tab labels.
- **Primary Deep**: `#4834D4` — Active/pressed states and high-emphasis accent text.
- **Soft Lavender Fill**: `#F0EDFF` — Soft tinted pill backgrounds, light interactive states, and active navigation items.

### Secondary & Utility Accents
- **Tech Blue**: `#0984E3` — Secondary interactive elements, links, and student service badges.
- **Electric Cyan**: `#00CEC9` — Campus life features, technical tags, and highlight tickers.

### Semantic Tones
- **Verified / Stipends / Success**: `#00B894` (Background tint: `#E6F8F4`, Border tint: `#A7EBDC`)
- **Pending / Action Required / Warning**: `#F39C12` (Background tint: `#FEF5E7`, Border tint: `#FCDCA8`)
- **Urgent / Exam Deadlines / Critical**: `#FF7675` (Background tint: `#FFF0F0`, Border tint: `#FFCECE`)

### Typography Colors
- **Primary Text**: `#1E2038` — Deep navy slate, maximizing readability while feeling softer than pure black.
- **Secondary Text**: `#5E6472` — Medium balanced slate for metadata, helper text, and secondary headers.
- **Muted / Placeholder**: `#9DA4B4` — Disabled states, icon outlines, and input watermarks.

## Typography

The type scale pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with **Inter** for functional interfaces, forms, and data.

### Structural Pairing
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers rounded geometric friendliness with purposeful structural weights (`600` to `800`). It establishes the contemporary startup aesthetic and keeps institutional communications approachable.
- **Body & Data (Inter)**: Built for operational neutrality, dense schedules, grade sheets, and marketplace descriptions. Its tall x-height guarantees legibility on low-spec Android devices common in student demographics.

### Currency Handling (₹)
- The Indian Rupee symbol must use regular system lining or standard Inter glyphs (`₹`), avoiding faux superscripts. For numerical tables, stipends, and canteen totals, always apply tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with `body-md-medium` or `headline-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt modular spacing grid** adhering to a 12-column responsive layout for desktop/tablet and a 4-column responsive grid for mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (≥1280px)**: 12-column fluid grid, `1200px` max content container, `margin`: `2rem` (or auto-centered), `gutter`: `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column fluid grid, `margin`: `1.5rem`, `gutter`: `1rem`. Side navigation transitions into an expandable drawer or compact icon rail.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout, `margin-mobile`: `1rem`, `gutter-mobile`: `1rem`. Secondary auxiliary panes collapse into bottom sheets.

### Spacing Principles
- **Internal Card Padding**: Minimum `space-md` (16px) on mobile, upgrading to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop to preserve the light, uncluttered feel.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Section groupings must use `space-xl` (40px) to prevent cognitive overload in data-heavy student dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **ambient tinted shadows** combined with **subtle tinted slate outlines**. Interfaces avoid murky grayscale drop shadows, favoring atmospheric lavender and navy diffusions.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FAFAFE` base, 0px offset, no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Input Groups)**: 
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #E8E7F3`
  - Shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 32, 56, 0.04), 0px 4px 16px -4px rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.06)`
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Interactive Card Hovers, Filters)**:
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #DFDCF0`
  - Shadow: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 32, 56, 0.06), 0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.10)`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Context Menus, Floating Navigation, Toasts)**:
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #D1CDED`
  - Shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 32, 56, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.08)`

### Micro-Transitions
- Card elevations lift smoothly upon cursor interaction: shift upward by `-2px` accompanied by a transition from **Level 1** to **Level 2** over `180ms ease-out`.

## Shapes

The visual style embraces friendly, approachable geometry through generous corner radii:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Inputs, segmented tabs, inner elements, and buttons.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: Standard containers, content modules, event cards, and bottom sheets.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Category tags, live status indicators, counter badges, and filter chips.
- **Strict Prohibition**: Sharp 0px corners are reserved exclusively for horizontal divider lines.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#6C5CE7` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`, height `42px` (`px-4`, `py-2`). Hover transitions to `#5842D8`. Active state deepens to `#4834D4`.
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, border `1.5px solid #E8E7F3`, text `#1E2038`. Hover transitions to border `#6C5CE7` with text `#6C5CE7`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent surface, text `#5E6472`. Hover fills `#F0EDFF` with text `#6C5CE7`.
- **Touch Target**: Touch surfaces never drop below `44px` height on mobile.

### 2. Status Chips & Pill Tags
- **Structure**: Height `26px`, padding `2px 10px`, border radius `9999px`, font `label-sm` (`600` weight).
- **Primary Pill**: Background `#F0EDFF`, text `#6C5CE7`, border `1px solid rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.15)`.
- **Verified / Stipend Pill**: Background `#E6F8F4`, text `#00B894`, border `1px solid #A7EBDC`. Must include a `6px` solid green dot before text.
- **Urgent Notice Pill**: Background `#FFF0F0`, text `#FF7675`, border `1px solid #FFCECE`.

### 3. Input Fields
- **Default State**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E8E7F3`, height `44px`, corner radius `0.5rem`, typography `body-md`. Inner padding `12px 16px`.
- **Focus State**: Border `#6C5CE7`, box shadow `0px 0px 0px 3px rgba(108, 92, 231, 0.15)`. Outline is disabled.
- **Prefix / Suffix Elements**: Integrated currency (`₹`), search icons, and dropdown arrows tinted `#5E6472`.

### 4. Cards
- **Specification**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E7F3`, corner radius `1rem`, padding `1.25rem` (mobile) to `1.5rem` (desktop).
- **Interactive Variants**: Feature a subtle top accent strip (e.g., `3px solid #6C5CE7` or `#00CEC9`) for specialized announcements, hackathons, or society updates.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox**: `20px × 20px`, corner radius `4px`, border `1.5px solid #D1CDED`. Checked state applies `#6C5CE7` fill with a white checkmark.
- **Radio Button**: `20px × 20px`, circular (`radius-full`), border `1.5px solid #D1CDED`. Selected state shows `#6C5CE7` ring with centered `8px` solid circle.

### 6. Campus-Specific Elements
- **Stipend / Price Callout**: Displayed in `headline-sm` with the Rupee sign (`₹`), styled as `₹25,000 / mo` where the currency and figure are weighted in bold navy `#1E2038` and period denominator uses `body-sm` in `#5E6472`.
- **Notice Feed Item**: Clean hairline split row with high-contrast date badge on the left (light lavender square) and notification subject on the right.
